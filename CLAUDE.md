# hostmenow: build brief for Claude Code

You are building **hostmenow**, an invite-only travel membership, as an **Expo / React Native** app (iPhone first) plus a one-page locked web landing. This first build is a **clickable prototype on mock data**: no real backend, payments, ID checks or maps API keys yet. It should look and move like the finished product.

Pitch line: **5 nights free. 5 days out.**

## The feeling (read before writing any UI)

A private club with an unmarked door: hard to get into, effortless once you're in. Jony Ive era Apple. Calm, confident, quiet luxury, never salesy.

- One primary action per screen. Onboarding shows one question or action per full screen.
- Photography carries the product. Text is secondary.
- No badges, banners (except the paused-membership one), confetti, gamification, upsells or promotional color.
- Hairline dividers, never boxes. No drop shadows except bottom sheets.
- Inside the app, keep Airbnb-familiar patterns: search, listing, book, trip.
- The nights bank is the only number that matters: always visible, never shouted.
- When in doubt, remove.

## Stack

- **Expo SDK (latest) + TypeScript + Expo Router** (file-based routes, typed routes on).
- **react-native-reanimated** for all motion and **react-native-gesture-handler**.
- **expo-image** for photos (with `contentPosition` for crops, see below).
- **@gorhom/bottom-sheet** for the booking sheet, filters and the "verifying ID" sheet.
- **expo-haptics**: light impact on primary actions, success notification on payment and booking.
- **expo-camera** for the ID, selfie and video-intro screens. A static dark placeholder is fine on simulator and web.
- **react-native-maps** with a grayscale custom style for Explore map and listing location. Web fallback: a static placeholder.
- **zustand** for mock app state (member, nights bank, bookings, onboarding progress).
- Fonts: the iOS system font (SF Pro) on iOS; **Inter 400/600** on web and Android via `@expo-google-fonts/inter`. Two weights only.
- **Mock these for now:** Sign in with Apple and Google, ID verification, Apple Pay and card payment, and the share sheet. Each one resolves after a short, realistic delay. Keep native-looking buttons, but put them behind a single `services/` layer so real providers (for example `expo-apple-authentication` and Stripe) can be swapped in later.
- Use pnpm unless the repo already uses something else.

## Tokens (full set in `theme.ts`)

- Colors: `bg #FFFFFF`, `bgSubtle #F5F5F4`, `ink #111111`, `inkSecondary #6B6B6B`, `inkTertiary #A3A3A3`, `line #E7E5E4`, `accent #8A7A63`, `danger #B42318`. Dark: `bg #0B0B0B`, `ink #F5F5F4`.
- Onboarding and the locked landing are **dark**. The app interior is **light**. B7 cross-fades from dark to light when "Start exploring" is tapped.
- Type: Display 40/44 600 (-2% tracking), Title 28/34 600, Heading 20/26 600, Body 17/24 400, Callout 15/20 400, Caption 13/18 400.
- Wordmark: "hostmenow", lowercase, semibold, -2% tracking.
- Spacing: 4pt grid; side margin 24. Radius: 12 cards and images, pill buttons and chips, 20 sheets.
- Sheet shadow only: `0 8 32 rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.
- Icons: SF Symbols style, 1.5px outline, filled only for the selected tab. Use `expo-symbols` on iOS with a lucide or custom SVG fallback elsewhere.

## Components to build first

Build these as shared components before the screens. The `design-reference/Components.dc.html` sheet shows them together.

- `PrimaryButton`: 56 tall pill, full width minus margins, ink fill with white label (inverted on dark). Scales to 0.97 on press and fires a light haptic.
- `TextButton`: ink Body Semibold.
- `PayButton`: black on light screens, white on dark screens.
- `ProgressLine`: 2px, animates width between steps, no step numbers.
- `ListingCard`: 4:5 photo, radius 12. Below it: name (Heading), region (Callout, secondary), then a price line, which is either `Free · cleaning $85` with "Free" in accent, or `$220` struck through in tertiary followed by `$110 night`.
- `NightsPill`: "5 nights", Callout Semibold on `bgSubtle`. Taps through to the Nights bank.
- `DateChips`: the next 5 days only, calculated from today. Selected chips use an ink fill; unavailable chips use tertiary.
- `Segmented`: List / Map and Upcoming / Past.
- `TabBar`: hairline top border and no tint.
- `Sheet`, `Avatar` (40 and 64), `VideoTile` (9:16, radius 12).

## Motion

- Step transitions: horizontal push, 350ms, spring with high damping and no bounce.
- Sheets rise from the bottom in 300ms.
- Onboarding and landing backgrounds: one photo drifting slowly (Ken Burns, 20s, alternate) under a black overlay. Use 60% by default; the landing uses about 40% on phones and 45% on desktop on `landing.jpg` because the photo is already dark.
- Invalid invite code: the field shakes once and the caption reads "That code isn't valid."
- Confirmed screen: the nights pill animates from 5 to 3.
- Nothing decorative.

Also: tabs, the nights pill opening the Nights bank, You opening Nights bank and Invites, Sign out returning to the landing, and the List / Map toggle.

Add a hidden dev menu (long-press the wordmark on Explore) to switch mock states:

- nights bank at 5 or 0 (0 shows every card at 50%)
- membership paused (banner: "Your membership is paused. Your 5 nights are safe." with a Resume button)
- ID still verifying (tapping Book shows the sheet "One moment. We're still checking your ID." and it resolves to success)

## Business rules (mock logic)

- Bookable only within 5 days of check-in, so the date chips always show today +1 through today +5.
- Each member gets 5 free nights a year; the guest pays only cleaning and taxes on those nights. After the free nights run out, nights are 50% of the home's retail rate. Free nights are used oldest first and expire 5 years after they are granted.
- "Use free nights" toggle in the booking sheet, on by default.
- Pricing follows Revision 03 (margin plan), from the versioned config in `src/config.ts` (`PRICING_CONFIGS`); never hardcode rates:
  - Membership $25 a month. A $20 booking fee per booking (free or paid), platform revenue, not pooled.
  - Pool = 45% of membership revenue + 25% of the platform fee. Platform keeps 15% of paid (50%) stays.
  - Host card fees (2.9%) come off paid-stay and cleaning payouts, never pool payouts.
  - Host figures come only from `estimate()` in `src/lib/estimate.ts`; booking and payout math from `src/lib/pricing.ts`.

## Open decisions (ask Landon, don't guess)

1. **The booking example doesn't add up.** Jordan has 5 free nights, yet the spec's breakdown charges 1 of 3 nights at 50% and the pill drops from 5 to 3. Implement the calculation as its own function (`priceStay`) so the rule is easy to change. Default for now: use all available free nights, then 50% for the rest, and flag this in the PR. Is there a per-stay cap on free nights?
2. **The accent color is slightly too faint.** #8A7A63 is just under the WCAG AA contrast minimum for the small "Free" label on white. Consider #7A6B55.
3. **Real services later:** auth provider, ID verification vendor, payments (Stripe with Apple Pay?), and where listings come from (the Hostshare API).
